The Seraph’s Ledger

 

A personal bundle of small quality-of-life tweaks for Vintage Story.



The Seraph’s Ledger gathers a handful of independent conveniences into one mod — a trader-tracking ledger, larger storage containers, inventory shortcuts, automatic enemy loot, a material swatch tool for builders, and a few audio/UI tweaks. Every runtime feature can be switched on or off individually from an in-game settings panel, so you can run the whole bundle or just the parts you want.

What it does

 

Ice-Cooled Cellars

Keep your food twice as long by stocking your cellar with ice.

Place blocks of ice (glacier ice, packed glacier ice — anything icy) in a proper cellar and the room chills below the vanilla 5°C cellar floor. The effect starts at 4 ice blocks and maxes out at 10, where the cellar behaves as if it were about −3°C — roughly double the shelf life of a perfect vanilla cellar. Nothing freezes; food just keeps longer.

- Placement is flexible: ice counts whether it's stacked inside the room or lining the walls, floor, or ceiling — wall lining keeps your storage space free.
- Ice walls now actually cool: vanilla treats ice as a non-cooling wall material (like wood!); with this mod, ice walls rate like stone or soil for the cellar bonus.
- Ice melts realistically: instead of vanishing at outdoor-summer speed, cellar ice melts based on the chilled room temperature — the more ice you stock, the colder the room and the slower each block melts. A fully chilled cellar loses roughly one block per ~17 hours of play, faster as the stock dwindles. In winter, cellar ice doesn't melt at all.
- No flooding: ice melting indoors simply vanishes — no water sources left behind in your cellar. Fully buried ice (like the outer layer of a double wall) is insulated and never melts.
- See it working: hover any container in the cellar — the tooltip shows your ice count and chill strength, and the "Stored food perish speed" numbers update to match.

The room must qualify as a vanilla cellar (small, enclosed, no exits). Thresholds, target temperature, and melt rate are tunable in ModConfig/seraphsledger/features.json; the whole feature can be toggled in the in-game settings panel (Ctrl+Shift+L).

Only want this one feature? It also ships on its own as the Ice Cellar mod — same mechanic, none of the rest of the bundle. Running both is safe from Seraph’s Ledger 1.15.2 onward: this mod detects Ice Cellar and hands the feature over, so nothing is applied twice. On 1.15.1 or older that hand-off doesn’t work — update, or switch ice-cooled cellars off here.

Material swatch tool

See a build in a different material before you commit to it. Aim at one corner and run /sw mark, then aim at the opposite corner and mark again — two marks at the same height read as a footprint and the top is taken from the rain map, so a whole building is bracketed without flying up to its roof. The area is highlighted so you can see what you caught. Press Ctrl+Shift+M: the materials found in the selection are on the left, what each can become is on the right with the number of blocks each swap would change. Apply, and Undo if you don’t like it.

  • Every finish of a material moves at once — cobble, bricks, polished, stairs and slabs all follow, and stair facings survive the swap. The choices come from the block registry rather than a built-in list, so modded stone and wood show up on their own and swaps that don’t exist are never offered.

  • Only blocks somebody placed are listed or swapped. Grass, soil, gravel, sand, ore, raw stone, water and plant life stay out of it, so the list holds your build rather than the ground under it — and restyling a wall never repaints the hillside behind it.

  • Chiselled blocks are re-materialed in place, keeping their carving.

  • Blocks holding contents or state — chests, doors — are skipped and reported rather than swapped and emptied.

  • Undo covers your last 25 swaps, newest first, from the dialog or /sw undo.

  • Chat equivalents — /sw list, /sw options [material], /sw swap <from> <to>, /sw unmark. Requires the controlserver privilege.

Only want this one feature? It also ships on its own as the Swatch mod — install it alongside this one and Seraph’s Ledger hands the feature over, the same way Ice Cellar works.

 

Trader ledger

Automatically records every trader whose Buy/Sell window you open — their location, name, type, and full buy/sell lists (prices and stock as of your last visit). Stored per-world; revisiting a trader updates their entry in place.

  • Map waypoints — a color-coded (by type) waypoint is dropped at each trader the first time you visit. .traders wp maps any not yet marked.

  • In-game browser — open with Ctrl+K (rebindable). Search by name, type, or item; sort by type/name/recent; click [show on map] to jump to that trader.

  • Chat — .traders lists everyone; .traders <text> shows full wares for matches; .traders export writes a Markdown summary to the mod data folder.

 

Large storage containers

Bigger variants of the vanilla containers, each crafted from two of the base container in a shapeless grid recipe. They keep the vanilla look, footprint, and sounds.

  • Large Trunk — 100 slots, crafted from 2× Trunk

  • Large Chest — 45 slots, crafted from 2× Chest

  • Large Labeled Chest — 45 slots, crafted from 2× Labeled Chest

  • Large Storage Vessel — 35 slots, crafted from 2× Storage Vessel

  • Large Basket — 21 slots, crafted from 2× Basket

 

Inventory quality-of-life

  • Ctrl + left-click any slot in an inventory dialog to dump every non-empty slot of that inventory into the open container(s).

  • Sort button in the player inventory — server-authoritative sort of your worn-bag contents.

  • Trash button in the player inventory — destroys the stack currently held on the cursor.

 

Combat / loot

  • Hostile creatures vanish 2 seconds after death and drop their loot automatically — no knife-harvesting required. Applies to anything in the vanilla hostile spawn group (drifters, locusts, bells, shivers, bowtorn, and hostile animals like wolves, bears, hyenas) plus modded mobs using that group. Drops match a full harvest (scaled by animalLootDropRate for non-mechanical mobs); harvesting the body yourself within the 2 seconds never duplicates loot.

 

Creative inventory

  • The search box is auto-focused when the creative inventory opens — start typing immediately. (While focused, the E key types into the search instead of closing the dialog — close with Escape or the X.)

 

Audio

  • Silenced character voice sounds — both player/seraph voices and the trader greeting.

 

Distance Challenge

  • Distance challenge — HUD travel budget that drains as you walk; can't move at 0. /steps add|set.

Requirements

  • Vintage Story (no specific minimum version pinned; built against current game APIs).

  • No other mod dependencies.

  • Singleplayer or multiplayer. The fast-despawn loot feature is server-side, so on a dedicated server it follows the server’s own config; install the mod on the server for that and the trader/container features to work.

Selecting or deselecting individual features

Press Ctrl+Shift+L (rebindable) or run .slconfig to open the settings panel. It has one switch per runtime feature:

  • Trader ledger

  • Ctrl+click move-all

  • Sort / Trash buttons

  • Creative search auto-focus

  • Enemy fast-despawn

  • Large Containers
  • Silenced Voices
  • Ice-cooled cellars
  • Material swatch tool
  • Distance challenge

Your choices are saved to ModConfig/seraphsledger/features.json. A restart is required for changes to take effect — these features are wired up at load time (Harmony patches, hotkeys, event handlers), so the panel warns you of this.
